# Woozly — PRODUCT.md

## Product

Woozly is an iOS social app: join the place you're already at, drop a plant with your intention for being there, and let Woozly AI find people at the same spot who actually match your vibe. Real places, real intentions, real conversations.

## Register

brand — marketing landing page. Design communicates the product's soul; conversion is a secondary outcome.

## Target users

Urban adults 18–35 who frequent cafes, parks, bars, and campuses. Curious, intentional, done with profile swiping. They're at interesting places constantly — Woozly makes them not strangers to the people next to them.

## Brand personality

Warm. Alive. Grounded. Intentional. Think: a handwritten note tucked into a plant at your regular coffee shop — intimate, discoverable, a little magical.

Anti-references: dating app aesthetics (Tinder/Hinge energy), corporate SaaS minimalism (Linear/Vercel black-grid), loud social media (TikTok maximalism), generic "social network" blue.

## Brand colors (committed)

- Primary violet: `oklch(40.8% 0.228 293)` (#6D28D9) — the brand's committed accent
- Sage green: `oklch(47% 0.09 155)` — plants, nature, place
- Background: near-white with a whisper of violet tint `oklch(97.5% 0.008 290)` — light, airy, not cream

## Typography

- Display: Poppins — brand committed, warm geometric
- Body: Figtree — warm rounded-geometric sans, not on reflex-reject list

## Key features (in order of emphasis)

1. **Drop a plant with intention** — pin a sticky note to a real location with your intention (open to conversation, deep work, book recs, etc.). This is the core differentiator.
2. **Woozly AI match** — AI reads everyone's intentions at the same place and calculates live compatibility %. Not an algorithm deciding who's hot — it finds who you'd actually want to talk to.
3. **Sticky notes on places** — anyone can leave/discover notes at a place; build connection with the spot itself over time, share notes with friends.
4. **Events** — host a gathering at the place directly from the app.
5. **Instagram share** — share your place card or plant to stories.

## Design principles

- Skeuomorphic product mockups: cards look like real app UI, sticky notes look physically present (rotation, paper texture, fold corner)
- Light mode throughout — warm near-white bg, white cards, violet and sage as accents
- Nature metaphors made literal: sticky notes ARE plants, leaf IS the logo mark
- Intro animation: leaf drops and becomes the "o" in the wordmark — one moment of personality before the page reveals
- No identical card grids; each feature section earns its own spatial logic
- Shadows carry depth, not borders — no border+shadow ghost-card combinations
